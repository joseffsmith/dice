# DM SECRETS: VESSMOUTH (spoilers, please don't read until the adventure's over!)

_Written before the adventure begins, so the plot stays fixed._

## The truth
- Vessmouth is a cold northern port and university town at the head of a fjord, beneath the **Hvitfell Glacier**. It lives on the Academy, fishing, and the
  **ice trade**: glacier ice cut and shipped south.
- Frozen deep in the glacier is a second Sleeper: the **Glass Whale**, a vast leviathan locked in ice for a thousand years. Unlike Saltmarrow's, **it still has both eyes**.
  Its right eye, the **Vessmouth Eye** (a pale blue-white orb the size of a man's head), is close to the ice face in a cavern the icecutters broke into last spring.
- The **Lantern Guild's plan**: cut the Eye out and carry it to the capital. The Grand Council is collecting Eyes to build a network of "Lanterns" that could
  steer the Sleepers, and so the seas and coasts of the whole Empire. (Crane's binding was a prototype.)
- **What removing the Eye does**: the Glass Whale stirs. Its heat (the Sleepers burn with cold fire) melts the glacier from within, and a **glacial outburst flood** bursts
  down the valley and destroys Vessmouth. The Academy's glaciologists suspect the ice is moving faster; the Guild has buried their reports.
- **Ending it**: the Litany of Saint Aurel has a northern variant, the *Rime Litany*, known only to the Academy's archives and to the icecutters' grandmothers.
  The same principle applies: an Eye sung brittle at its own Sleeper and shattered puts it to sleep for ever. **But** this one is still IN the Whale's head:
  shattering it in place ends the Whale. Removing it first means you'd need to carry it back to the Whale.

## The clock
- The Guild's ice-hulled ship, the ***Gilded Lantern***, arrives in **8 days** to collect the Eye. The cutting team works night and day.
  - Day 6: they break the Eye free of the ice (unless stopped). The glacier starts groaning, and the river runs warm.
  - Day 8: the *Gilded Lantern* sails with the Eye. Day 9: the outburst flood.
- Sable arrives on **day 1**.

## Factions and NPCs
- **Lady Seraphine Ashgrove**: the Lantern Guild's envoy. Elegant, witty, ruthless, about 30. **A con artist herself** (she was born in the capital's slums
  as "Sera Ash" and conned her way into the Guild). She's Sable's mirror and rival: Deception +8, Insight +6, Perception +4. She has **Crane's report**,
  describing "a half-elf thief with an actor's face", but no likeness. She'd recognise Tib's red scarf from the description.
  Secret: she doesn't know the Eye will destroy the town (the Guild lied to her too). Shown proof, she might turn. Use the *noble* stats
  plus a hidden **hand crossbow** with drow-style sleep venom (Con DC 13 or unconscious).
- **Provost Hollis Varne**: head of the Academy. Bought by the Guild (they're paying off the Academy's debts). Pompous and frightened.
- **Doctor Mirren Thale**: Magister Orrin Thale's younger sister, a glaciologist at the Academy. Sharp-tongued, practical, tired. Orrin wrote to her from his ship:
  *"A half-elf and his friends saved Saltmarrow. If they come north, trust them."* She has the **glacier measurements** (the ice is moving 3x faster) and knows
  of the *Rime Litany* in the sealed archive. **She meets the brig on the dock.**
- **Countess Brynja Halvard**: the hereditary ruler of Vessmouth. Old, proud, broke, and desperate for Guild gold. She's not evil. She'd act if shown proof.
- **The Icecutters** (the "Rime-folk"): hard labourers who cut the glacier ice. Their foreman is **Ragna Ironhand** (huge, quiet, with a missing finger). Their grandmothers'
  faith calls the frozen beast "**Old Grandmother**" and forbids waking her. They were hired by the Guild and are deeply uneasy; some are going mad from **dreams of cold fire**.
  The eldest grandmother, **Mor Ylva**, knows the Rime Litany by heart.
- **The Guild's muscle**: 8 Guild guards (*guard* stats) plus **Captain Dask**, a mercenary (*veteran*), at the ice cavern camp. There's also a Guild agent in
  the harbour office, a clerk named **Pell Arnquist** (no relation to Mother Pell), who checks arrivals against Crane's description.

## Opening (day 1)
- The brig docks in sleet. The harbour clerk Arnquist is questioning arrivals ("Half-elves? Red scarves?"). Seraphine watches from a carriage.
- **Mirren Thale** is waiting on the dock with her brother's letter, looking for "a half-elf and his friends". She is not subtle.
- Arnquist's checks: Deception or Disguise vs his Insight +2 / passive Perception 12. Tib's red scarf is a tell (Seraphine's Perception +4 to spot it from the carriage, if visible).
